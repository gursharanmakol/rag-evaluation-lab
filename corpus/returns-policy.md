# Returns Policy

## Return window for new products {#standard}

You can return a new TechNova product within 15 days of delivery. This applies to headphones, earbuds, speakers, and accessories bought from techNova.com or the TechNova app. The 15 days start on the delivery date shown in Orders, not the order date.

To qualify, the product must be in its original packaging, with all cables, cases, and manuals included. Gift returns follow the same window, counted from the delivery date to the person who placed the order.

If your return window has already closed, a product that stopped working may still be covered by the Limited Warranty. Contact support and we'll check which option applies to your order.

## Opened and used items {#opened}

Opened products can be returned within the same window if they're undamaged and complete. Headphones and earbuds must include every ear tip and cushion they came with. For hygiene reasons, earbuds with missing ear tips can't be accepted.

Opened electronics have a 10 percent restocking fee, which is taken from your refund. Unopened products in sealed packaging are refunded in full.

If an opened product arrives back damaged, scratched, or with parts missing, we may send it back to you or offer a partial refund. Our team will email you photos and an explanation before deciding.

## How to start a return {#how-to}

Open Orders, choose the item, and select Return item. Pick a reason, add a short note if you like, then print the prepaid return label. Return shipping is free within the United States.

Pack the product securely, ideally in its original box, and drop it off with the carrier within 14 days of printing the label. You can track the parcel from the Orders page.

When the return arrives, our warehouse inspects it within 3 business days. You'll get an email when the inspection is complete, and your refund is processed under the Refund Policy. Most banks show the refund within 5 to 10 business days.

## Items that can't be returned {#exceptions}

A few items can't be returned once they're delivered. These include gift cards, digital gift codes, downloadable software and apps, personalized or engraved products, and replacement ear tips and cushions once the package is opened.

Products marked final sale on the product page can't be returned either. The final sale label is shown before checkout and again in your order confirmation email.

If a non-returnable product arrives damaged or not as described, contact support within 7 days of delivery. We'll replace it or refund it, and you won't need to send it back in most cases.
