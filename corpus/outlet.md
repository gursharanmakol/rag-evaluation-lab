# TechNova Outlet

## Outlet purchase terms {#terms}

The TechNova Outlet sells inspected, graded units at a lower price, including open-box and refurbished stock. Quantities are limited, and an item in your cart isn't reserved until you check out. Outlet orders ship from a separate warehouse and usually arrive in 4 to 6 business days.

Outlet purchases can be sent back for a refund within 7 days of delivery. After that, the sale is final, though the item is still covered for defects under a 90-day warranty.

Outlet items can't be combined with promotions, partner gift cards, or price matching. Each listing shows the grade and photos of the actual unit or one in the same grade.
