# EB-20 Earbuds

## Returns and warranty {#returns}

You can return EB-20 earbuds within 15 days of delivery for a full refund. Because earbuds go in your ears, they must come back with every ear tip size and the charging case, and the ear tips must be clean. Start the return from the Orders page.

EB-20 earbuds are covered by TechNova's 1-year limited warranty. If one earbud loses sound or the case stops charging, you can make a warranty claim from your order.

Lost earbuds and lost charging cases aren't covered, but single replacement earbuds can be ordered from support at a reduced price.
