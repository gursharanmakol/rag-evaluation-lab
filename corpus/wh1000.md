# WH-1000 Headphones

## Product overview {#overview}

The WH-1000 is TechNova's wireless over-ear headphone with active noise cancellation. It offers up to 30 hours of playback with noise cancellation on, and a 10-minute charge gives about 3 hours of listening.

The WH-1000 connects over Bluetooth 5.3 and can stay paired with two devices at the same time. Controls are on the right ear cup, and the companion app lets you adjust the equalizer and noise-cancellation level.

In the box you'll find the headphones, a carrying case, a USB-C charging cable, and a 3.5 mm audio cable for wired listening on flights or older devices.

## Care and troubleshooting {#care}

To clean the ear cushions and headband, wipe them with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Don't use alcohol or cleaning sprays. Replacement cushions are sold separately on the accessories page.

If the WH-1000 won't pair, hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue, then select it in your device's Bluetooth settings. If it still won't connect, reset it by holding the power and noise-cancellation buttons together for 10 seconds.

To keep the battery healthy, charge it fully before first use and store the headphones at about half charge if you won't use them for a month or more.

Changed your mind about your WH-1000? You can return the headphones within 15 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're in the original packaging with the case and both cables. Start the return from the Orders page.

Battery wear, worn ear cushions, and accidental damage aren't covered. Replacement cushions and cables are sold on the accessories page.
