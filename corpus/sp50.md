# SP-50 Speaker

## Returns and warranty {#returns}

You can return the SP-50 speaker within 15 days of delivery for a full refund. Pack it in the original box with the charging cable and the strap, and start the return from the Orders page. Return shipping is free.

The SP-50 is covered by TechNova's 1-year limited warranty. A speaker with a failed driver, a battery that won't hold a charge, or buttons that stop responding can be repaired or replaced.

Water damage beyond the speaker's IP67 rating, drops, and cracked housings aren't covered by the warranty. Spare straps are sold separately.
