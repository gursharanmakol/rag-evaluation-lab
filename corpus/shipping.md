# Shipping FAQ

## Delivery times {#delivery}

Standard shipping is free on orders over 35 dollars and usually arrives in 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping, available at checkout, arrives in 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day.

You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse. If the tracking hasn't updated for 3 business days, contact support and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

We ship to all 50 states, Puerto Rico, and US military addresses, including APO and FPO. We don't currently ship outside the United States.

## Return shipping and labels {#returns}

Return shipping is free for orders delivered in the United States. Your prepaid label is created when you start a return in Orders, and you can print it or show a QR code at a drop-off point.

Labels expire 14 days after they're created. If yours expires before you drop off the parcel, start the return again to get a new label. You don't need to cancel the old one.

If you're returning more than one item, you can pack them together under one label. Use any sturdy box if you no longer have the original packaging. Cover old shipping labels and barcodes so the carrier scans the right one.
