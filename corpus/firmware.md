# WH-1000 Firmware Changelog

## Version 3.2 {#v3.2}

Firmware 3.2 for the WH-1000 was released in January 2026. It fixes noise cancellation briefly turning off when you switch between two connected devices, and it fixes a rare issue where the right ear cup stopped responding to taps.

It also improves battery reporting in the companion app, which now shows the remaining charge in 5 percent steps instead of 10.

Version 3.2 installs automatically through the companion app when the headphones are charging and connected to your phone. The update takes about 15 minutes. Don't turn off the headphones until the light stops flashing.

## Version 3.1 {#v3.1}

Firmware 3.1 for the WH-1000 was released in October 2025. It adds a low-latency mode for video and games, which you can turn on in the companion app under Sound settings.

It also fixes a problem where the voice prompts were too loud at low volume, and it shortens the time the headphones take to reconnect after they wake from sleep.

Version 3.1 is required before you can install later updates. If your headphones show an older version, update to 3.1 first, then check again for newer firmware.
