# Limited Warranty

## What the warranty covers {#coverage}

TechNova headphones, earbuds, and speakers come with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. It covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, for example a WH-1000 that stops charging, an earbud that loses sound, or a speaker with a failed driver.

The warranty doesn't cover accidental damage such as drops or liquid damage, normal wear such as worn cushions or scratches, or damage from unauthorized repairs.

If we confirm a defect, we'll repair or replace the product at no cost. A replacement is covered for the rest of the original warranty, or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## How to make a warranty claim {#claims}

Open Orders, select the product, and choose Warranty claim. Describe the problem and when it started. We may ask for a short video, or suggest troubleshooting steps first, such as a reset or a firmware update.

You'll need your order number or proof of purchase. Products bought from other retailers are covered too, as long as you can show the receipt.

Once a claim is approved, we send a prepaid shipping label. Repairs usually take 7 to 10 business days from the day we receive the product. Replacements ship as soon as the defective unit is scanned at our warehouse.
