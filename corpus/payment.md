# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods {#methods}

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, as well as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. TechNova gift cards can be used on their own or combined with one other payment method.

Your card is charged when the order ships, not when you place it. For pre-orders, we charge when the product becomes available and ships.

If a payment is declined, check that the billing address matches the one your bank has on file. Some banks block online purchases over a set amount, so you may need to approve the charge in your banking app. Business customers can ask support about paying by invoice.
